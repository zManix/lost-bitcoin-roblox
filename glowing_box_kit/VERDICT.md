# Find The Box → Roblox: verdict

Researched 2026-10-26: the Steam page, all 16 screenshots, both trailers frame by frame (35 s + 76 s), and a live
Roblox market check.

## What Find The Box is
- Steam, by Taco Games, releasing **Nov 4, 2026** (unreleased, no reviews yet).
- A box is hidden inside a cube of 1 million blocks. You punch your way in to find it, first person.
- Slow and cozy, or roguelite: blocks drop tokens → level up → pick 1 of 3 cards (18 spells, 100+ perks, companions).
- Normal (closer/further hints) or Hard (none); upgrades on/off; 4 cube sizes; Boxless mode; Daily Cube; seeds.
- Speedrun timer plus global leaderboards (today, week, month, all time).
- Buried "special" shrine rooms with odd little story lines; wall-run and wall-jump movement.
- **The look:** inked comic-style hands, a candlelit spa hall, block tunnels whose color changes with depth (brown →
  pink → yellow → blue → green), and a glowing crystal box.

## Verdict: 8.5/10, the strongest pick of all five concepts
- ✅ **Proven core:** Needle reached 86K with the same "find the one thing, beat your time" loop.
- ✅ **Fixes Needle's weakness:** level-up builds, a Daily Cube, leaderboards, cube sizes, Specials, a persistent cube,
  plus our Box Buddies and co-op cubes. That's the retention Needle doesn't have.
- ✅ **Nobody has it on Roblox**, and the Steam launch on Nov 4 can drive "find the box" searches. Be first.
- ✅ **A strong look you can copy** (mood, palette, light), with real reference frames in `style_refs/`.
- ⚠️ **Risk 1, tech:** a million breakable blocks on phones. Fix: server byte grid + client-only greedy-meshed
  chunks, proven first in milestone M2 with a performance test.
- ⚠️ **Risk 2, fading** like Needle. Fix: the Daily Cube, leaderboards, Box Buddies, weekly updates.
- ⚠️ **Risk 3, the Steam version may never go viral.** It doesn't matter: Needle proved the loop without it.

**Name:** FIND THE GLOWING BOX (free on Roblox; still matches "find the box" searches; not the Steam title).
