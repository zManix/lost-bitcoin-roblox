# Front-page art prompts: Find the Glowing Box

Use them with Gemini image (newest model). Set the aspect ratio: 1:1 for the logo/icon, 16:9 for thumbnails.
Generate 4 of each and pick the best. Look at each one at phone size (zoomed out) before choosing.

## Logo (wordmark, text allowed here; regenerate until every letter is spelled right)
```
A bold game logo wordmark reading exactly "FIND THE GLOWING BOX" in chunky rounded 3D letters, stacked on two lines:
"FIND THE" small on top, "GLOWING BOX" big below. Warm cream letters with a thick dark-plum outline and a soft gold
bevel, a small glowing mint-green cube replacing the letter O in "BOX", soft mint light spilling onto the letters,
tiny golden sparkles around it. Clean, playful, toy-like, high contrast, readable at small size. Plain transparent-
looking dark background, centered, nothing else in the image.
```

## Icon (512×512; the most important image: it's what phone players see)
```
Square game icon. A giant cube made of thousands of small chunky blocks in layered colors (warm brown, rose pink, gold,
sky blue, mint), floating in a dark candlelit hall. One corner of the cube is cracked open and a bright glowing
mint-green box shines from deep inside, beams of mint light bursting out through the cracks. Small golden coins and
tumbling little blocks fly out of the crack. Stylized 3D render, chunky toy-like shapes, soft candle glow, strong
rim light, very high contrast, one clear focal point in the center, readable at tiny size. No text.
```

## Use them right
- Icon variants to A/B: the icon above, and a version where the glowing box sits alone in a hole in the blocks. Run
  them as an experiment in the Creator Dashboard if your account has it; otherwise switch weekly and compare
  click-through rate.
- Thumbnail order: 1 (hero) first, then 2, 5, 4, 3.
- Title text on thumbnails is optional: add it yourself in Canva/Photopea, short ("1 IN A MILLION"), never on the icon.
- Check every image: no extra fingers, no garbled letters, no real brands, nothing scary.
