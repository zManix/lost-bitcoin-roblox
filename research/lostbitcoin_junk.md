# Lost Bitcoin (Steam 5427110) and the junk/scrap/furnace genre: research report
Date of research: 2026-10-25. Sources are linked inline. Anything not directly confirmed is marked [UNVERIFIED]. Figures labelled [ESTIMATE] are my own calculations.

---

## 0. TL;DR
- **Lost Bitcoin is a solo or tiny project with about two weeks of public history.** The YouTube channel was created on 2026-10-06, the trailer was uploaded 2026-10-13 to 15, and the store assets were last updated 2026-10-22. It has about 105 to 109 Steam followers, no news posts, no demo, no Next Fest entry, a 69 MB build, Windows only, and 9 languages. The developer discloses that **generative AI was used for "almost all" content.** The YouTube Shorts and TikTok push is small but working: about 1.3 to 1.5K views per Short, 242 TikTok followers and 12.6K TikTok likes.
- **Its design is One Man's Trash (OMT) plus A Game About Digging A Hole (AGADAH), with a furnace and a deck factory added.** OMT already did the "lost crypto drive in a junkyard" premise with a vacuum, buried vehicles and locations, and a laptop shop. AGADAH did the minimalist dig, sell, upgrade loop and sold 1M+ copies.
- **Furnace-burning-for-money is proven on Roblox but untested as a premium Steam core loop.** On Roblox, *Feed the Fire* has 3.09M visits, 7.8K concurrent players and 98% likes in under 3 months. *burn stuff for money* has 9.6M visits, *Throw stuff for money* 39.5M, and *feed the pit* 1.57M. On Steam, "smelt or burn scrap" appears only as a sub-step inside junkyard sims such as Under The Rust and Recycling Center Simulator.
- **A Roblox clone of this exact premise already exists.** *Find The Lost Bitcoin* by LittleBitCoin has one real USB among fakes, buried in a landfill, and you sell scrap to a recycler. It was created 2026-09-17 and has about 30K visits and 84% likes. That predates the Steam page assets.

## 2. Direct ancestors

### 2.1 A Game About Digging A Hole (AGADAH), Steam 3244220
- Store: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3244220/. Released **Feb 7, 2025**, **$4.99** ("Costs you only one coffee!"). Steam now lists the developer as **DoubleBee**, with rokaplay select and Drillhounds publishing. Wikipedia says "Cyberwave, a German studio" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Game_About_Digging_a_Hole). The creator is "Ben", who built it in spare time while working on *Solarpunk*.
- Built in UE5 in **about 14 days** over a holiday break. The terrain tech was based on Motherload (Wikipedia).
- **Sales:** 100K+ wishlists before launch, 250K+ copies in week one, and **1M+ by Aug 1 2025** (Steam post "Mobile Port, 1 million copies sold"; PCGamesN, 2025-08-03). Later coverage says "millions" (GamingOnLinux). SteamSpy owner bracket: 500K to 1M.
- **Reviews:** 19,023 total. English: 90% of 11,512 positive (Very Positive). Last 30 days: 79% of 236 (Mostly Positive). Metacritic is "generally favorable"; OpenCritic shows 52% recommend.
- **Ports:** iOS and Android (via Headup, Aug 2025), consoles Dec 9 2025 (Xbox, PS5, Switch), and Game Pass in Dec 2025. An eBook, *A Tale About Digging a Hole*, followed in Dec 2025.
- The developer called out clones: "copycats … especially on mobile, and even on some consoles … copied our concept almost 1:1." *Hole Digging Master* on PS/Switch is one such clone.

**Mechanics** (Wikipedia and Steam guides https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3424935922 and ?id=3454148773):
- You dig in the backyard of a newly bought house. The 7 ore tiers and sell values are Stone 1, Coal 2, Iron 5, Silver 20, Gold 50, Platinum 100 and Diamond 250. You sell at the barn. Rainbow ore is a rare collectible worth nothing.
- There are 4 upgrade lines: **Shovel** (dig radius; at level 6 it becomes a hold-to-dig drill), **Inventory**, **Battery** (powers both shovel and jetpack; running out kills you; recharge costs rise with upgrades) and **Jetpack**.
- Consumables are **Dynamite ($100)**, needed for unbreakable rocks, and lamps. Lava rocks deal damage.
- A small mine dungeon holds a chest. Two keys hidden underground open a garage crate.
- **Moles** guard the bottom. They are fast, cannot be dynamited and give you no defence, so you dodge them.
- **Ending:** at -100 m you find the mine, follow it and grab the **treasure**. After that the game "changes genre, disables all game mechanics". You lose your tools, and a short story beat follows: a landlord buys land, finds the mine and the giant moles, escapes, then crosses the address off his list. Beating the game unlocks an **achievement mode** with garage shelves and golden tools for collecting everything.
- There are 10 achievements: TREASURE, I AM SHOVEL! (4,000 digs), Old Crate, Long Fall (4 s), BOOOOM! (100 dynamite), Rainbow (5), Moneymaker ($20K), Stonemason (100 stones), Wasted (50 ores) and The Fastest (beat it in under 30 minutes).
- No guide or wiki lists exact upgrade costs.
- **Length:** the main path takes about 40 to 60 minutes (speedruns under 30 minutes). Digging out the whole yard takes 5 to 10 hours.

**Review themes** (https://steamcommunity.com/app/3244220/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english and the positivereviews equivalent):
- Negative: "ends just as it starts becoming interesting" (0.8 h). It is short (1 to 2 hours). The ending "changes genre, disables all game mechanics". "money becomes functionally useless once you've gotten the upgrades". Upgrades are "extremely limited". The early game is slow and clicker-like. Dug terrain has no physics. Achievements require a tedious replay. There is too little to discover underground.
- Positive: Motherload nostalgia, relaxing, "childhood dream", satisfying loop. Players praise digging out the entire yard, and some ask for an endless mode.
- **Lesson for Lost Bitcoin:** players want more upgrades, more discoveries and an endless or post-game mode. They hate an ending that takes away their tools and upgrades that stop mattering.

- Implication: the AGADAH space gets a well-funded co-op entry on **Dec 4, 2026**, which will dominate "digging game" attention through the holidays.

### 2.3 One Man's Trash (OMT), Steam 3692070, the closest ancestor
- Store: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3692070/. Solo dev **Jony Pazu Games** (UE). Demo May 12 2025. Released **Jul 23, 2025**. **$6.85** (US). 14 languages.
- **Reviews:** 84% of 314 positive (Very Positive). SteamSpy owner bracket: 0 to 20K. [ESTIMATE] At 30 to 60 copies per review, that is roughly 9K to 19K copies. Steam followers are about 1,451 to 1,483.
- **Press:** PC Gamer, 2025-07-26: "Remember the guy who lost a bitcoin wallet worth millions in a landfill? There's a game about that now". TheGamer: https://www.thegamer.com/one-mans-trash-game-inspired-lost-500-million-bitcoin-hard-drive-landfill/. Both tie it to James Howells's 7,500-8,000 BTC hard drive.
- **Full feature list** (dev's Steam post, 2025-07-21, via the Steam News API):
  - Vacuum tool for "digging" trash. A central **rope** to climb out, with rope length as an upgrade. An endless pit.
  - **Laptop hub with 3 sites:** Junkbay (sell), Junkazon (buy upgrades) and Junkasino (gamble).
  - Upgrades: Vacuum Strength, Vacuum Size, Vacuum Capacity, Inventory Space and Rope Length. One-time tools are lamps and scanners (radar).
  - **62 collectibles:** 30 normal trash types, 20 rare items (Einstein's Brain, Moon pebble, Holy Grail, a "cursed cartridge" in the style of the E.T. Atari cartridge), and **12 buried locations: trains, cars, mine shafts and a spaceship with a teleporter**. Special items include double jump and a shop-discount timer.
  - 3 trophy containers, pedestals, vacuum skins and 25 achievements.
  - Hazards: fast angry **worms** and TNT traps.
  - **Main goal: find the lost hard drive (7,000 PitCoin).** The drive sits near the spaceship's nose, through the worm tunnels. At the laptop you choose the money or a mystery box that gives a worm pet. The "Edge of Existence" portal is at the pit's end, and there is a giant rideable worm (Shai-Hulud) deep down (achievement guide https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3535360963).
  - Depth milestones: 50, 100 and 150 m.
- **Game Modes update (2025-07-27):** Cozy (no enemies, coupons never expire), Classic, and Abyss (no rope, harder enemies, plus a "No Way Up" achievement). The pet worm now eats trash from the dustbag.
- **Review themes:**
  - Negative: worms and jumpscares ("The worms ruined it", "coming after you every 5 seconds"). Flat upgrades ("Everything just says 'Capacity: +5%'", "Upgrades are tiny and lack impact"). Copying complaints ("basically a carbon copy of the game about digging a hole", "no jetpack"). Too long for its variety. Crashes and lost saves. No way to buy health. Players expected a zen game "like Power Wash".
  - Positive: "the urge to keep playing is ridiculously strong", the "deeper you go, the weirder it gets" mystery, pop-culture easter eggs, a non-scripted ending, collectibles and unlocks. Players often call it "short but fun" at 3 to 8 hours, and completionists go to 20 to 50 hours.
- **Lesson for Lost Bitcoin:** Lost Bitcoin repeats OMT's premise: lost crypto drive, junkyard, vacuum, buried vehicle or ship, computer on the surface, crates. **"Clone of OMT and AGADAH" is the default criticism it will face.** OMT's own failure points are flat percentage upgrades, unavoidable enemies and save bugs. Lost Bitcoin's 60+ node skill tree and its factory layer are the obvious differentiators. Its "identical USB sticks you must check at the computer" mechanic is new versus OMT's single hard drive, and it acts like a lottery or gacha loop.

**Roblox takeaways:**
- "Find the one real item in a pile" games (Find The Lost Bitcoin, Find The Golden Ticket, Clear the Junkyard) are a September–October 2026 micro-trend, but each sits around 18K to 30K visits.
- The hits pair digging with a **reveal moment** (Dig & Clean: spray to reveal), a **collection or museum**, or **session-based escape or treasure runs of 30 to 60 minutes** (Dig to Escape, Dig the Backyard).
- *Feed the Fire* shows that a furnace loop needs a **pressure timer** (the storm) and **automation spawners** to retain players.
- Lost Bitcoin's identical USB sticks checked at a computer are a reveal mechanic, close to Dig & Clean's spray-to-reveal and well suited to Roblox.

## 5. Is furnace-burning-for-money proven?
- **Roblox: yes, in 2026.** *Feed the Fire* has 3.09M visits, about 7.8K concurrent players and 98% likes. *feed the pit* has 1.57M visits with automation. *Throw stuff for money* has 39.5M and *burn stuff for money* 9.6M (older, lower quality). The successful versions add (a) escalating fuel types, (b) a timer or threat (the storm), (c) a big reveal-as-you-buy upgrade tree and (d) auto-feeders.
- **Steam: not as a core loop.** Burning or smelting is a processing station inside junkyard or recycling sims (Under The Rust's smelting, Recycling Center's production, Keep Digging's smelter). AGADAH and OMT sell at a barn or laptop. Lost Bitcoin's furnace is functionally a "sell point" with physical delivery: carry the scrap or shoot it from the vacuum. That is a juicier version of a proven sell step, not a new economy.
- **Risks inherited from ancestors:**
  - Upgrades that become irrelevant (AGADAH) or flat percentage gains (OMT).
  - An ending that removes tools (AGADAH).
  - Unavoidable enemies (OMT worms).
  - Save and crash bugs (OMT, Junkyard Simulator).
  - Being perceived as a copy (OMT was called a "carbon copy" of AGADAH, and Lost Bitcoin will be called a copy of both).
