## Takeaways
1. The second-to-second is PHYSICAL: pick, pack, tip out, vacuum, magnet-shoot — every tool is a new way to move MORE junk into the fire faster. That "throughput escalation" is the core fun.
2. Weight gates ("Too heavy: 55 kg (carry strength 12 kg)") create desire for upgrades with zero text.
3. The stick reader's ROLLING-DIGITS reveal is the dopamine beat (near-miss "697,183 → 45").
4. Treasure has a big visual signature (blue light beam). Finder upgrades (X-ray, crate/safe/gold/wreck finder) turn digging into searching.
5. The factory is a Satisfactory-lite with SORTING by material — logic puzzle, not just pads.
6. ~64 upgrades, early costs 15–150, mid 2.5K–35K; coin values ~0.6–1 coin per kg early.
